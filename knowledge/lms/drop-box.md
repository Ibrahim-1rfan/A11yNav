## LMS
1. Open {course} from the course tabs at the top.
2. Select Drop Box in the left menu.
3. Next to your own folder, which has your name, select Actions.
4. Select Upload Files.
5. Select Drop files to upload, or click here to browse, and choose your file. You can also drag the file into the box.
6. Select Continue at the bottom of the page. Your file appears in your folder.
7. Only you and the course's instructors can see files in your Drop Box.
