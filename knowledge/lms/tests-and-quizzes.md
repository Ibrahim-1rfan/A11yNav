## LMS
1. Open {course} from the course tabs at the top.
2. Select Tests & Quizzes in the left menu.
3. Under Take an Assessment, select a quiz your instructor has opened to start it.
4. Your finished quizzes are listed under Submitted Assessments, with their scores and feedback when available.
