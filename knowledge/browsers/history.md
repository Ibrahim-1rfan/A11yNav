## Edge
1. Press Ctrl and H to open History.
2. Scroll or type in its search box to find the page, then select it.
3. If you just closed the tab, Ctrl, Shift and T brings it back.

## Chrome
1. Press Ctrl and H, or select More, then History, then History.
2. Select the page from the list, or use the search box at the top.
3. To delete history, select More, then Delete browsing data, choose how much, and select Delete data.

## Firefox
1. Press Ctrl and H to open the history sidebar, or Ctrl, Shift and H for the Library window.
2. Type in the search box or browse by date, then select the page.
3. To clear recent history, press Ctrl, Shift and Delete.
