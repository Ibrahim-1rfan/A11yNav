## Chrome
1. Select Close, the X on the tab you are on at the top of the window.
2. Or press Ctrl and W to close it from the keyboard.
3. Closed it by mistake? Press Ctrl, Shift and T to bring it back.

## Edge
1. Select Close tab, the X on the tab you are on at the top of the window.
2. Or press Ctrl and W to close it from the keyboard.
3. Closed it by mistake? Press Ctrl, Shift and T to bring it back.
