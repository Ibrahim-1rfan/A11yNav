## Chrome
1. Select Back, the arrow at the top left next to the address bar.
2. Or press Alt and the Left arrow to go back from the keyboard.
3. To go forward again, select Forward next to it, or press Alt and the Right arrow.

## Edge
1. Select Back, the arrow at the top left next to the address bar.
2. Or press Alt and the Left arrow to go back from the keyboard.
3. To go forward again, press Alt and the Right arrow.
