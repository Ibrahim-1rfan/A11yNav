## Edge
1. Open Settings and more (three dots, top right), then Settings.
2. Choose Appearance and, under Overall appearance, select Dark.
3. For stronger contrast everywhere, turn on Windows high contrast: press Left Alt, Left Shift and Print Screen together, or in Windows 11 go to Start, Settings, Accessibility, Contrast themes and choose a theme.
4. Edge follows the Windows contrast theme automatically.

## Chrome
1. Open a New Tab page with Ctrl and T.
2. At the bottom right, select Customize Chrome.
3. Under Appearance, choose Dark, Light, or Device to follow Windows.
4. For high contrast, use the Windows contrast themes in Start, Settings, Accessibility, Contrast themes.

## Firefox
1. Open the menu (three lines, top right) and choose Settings.
2. Select Appearance on the left, go to Website appearance and choose Dark, Light or System.
3. For your own text and background colours, go to Settings, Accessibility, and under Website contrast choose Custom, then Manage Colors.
4. Automatic under Website contrast follows the Windows high contrast setting.
