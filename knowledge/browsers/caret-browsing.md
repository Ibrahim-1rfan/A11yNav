## Edge
1. Press F7 to turn caret browsing on or off. A text cursor appears on the page.
2. Move with the arrow keys, Home, Page Up and Page Down. Hold Shift while moving to select text.
3. To keep it on all the time, open Settings and more, Settings, Accessibility, and turn on Navigate pages with a text cursor.
4. Press Tab and Shift and Tab to move between links and buttons. F6 moves between the tabs, address bar and page.
5. For a clearer focus box, turn on Show a high visibility outline on the focused object in the same Accessibility settings.

## Chrome
1. Press F7 to turn caret browsing on or off.
2. Move the cursor with the arrow keys. Hold Shift while moving to select text.
3. Press Tab and Shift and Tab to move between links and buttons, and F6 to jump to the address bar.

## Firefox
1. Press F7 to turn caret browsing on or off.
2. Move the cursor with the arrow keys. Hold Shift while moving to select text.
3. Press Tab and Shift and Tab to move between links and buttons. F6, Alt and D, or Ctrl and L jump to the address bar.
