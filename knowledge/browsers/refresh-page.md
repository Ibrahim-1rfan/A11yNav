## Chrome
1. Select Reload, the circular arrow at the top left next to the Back arrow.
2. Or press F5, or Ctrl and R, to reload from the keyboard.
3. If the page still looks wrong, press Ctrl, Shift and R to reload it without the saved copy.

## Edge
1. Select Refresh, the circular arrow at the top left next to the Back arrow.
2. Or press F5, or Ctrl and R, to refresh from the keyboard.
3. If the page still looks wrong, press Ctrl, Shift and R to reload it without the saved copy.
