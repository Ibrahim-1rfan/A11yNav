## Edge
1. Press Ctrl and F (or F3) to open Find on page.
2. Type the word you are looking for. Matches are highlighted.
3. Press Ctrl and G, or Enter, to jump to the next match.
4. Press Escape to close the find bar.

## Chrome
1. Press Ctrl and F (or F3) to open the find bar.
2. Type the word. Matches are highlighted.
3. Press Ctrl and G, or Enter, to go to the next match.
4. Press Escape to close it.

## Firefox
1. Press Ctrl and F to open Find in This Page.
2. Type the word. Matches are highlighted.
3. Press F3 or Ctrl and G for the next match, and Shift and F3 for the previous one.
4. Press Escape to close it.
