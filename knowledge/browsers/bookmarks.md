## Edge
1. Edge calls bookmarks favorites. Select Add this page to favorites, the star in the address bar, then select Done.
2. Or press Ctrl and D to save the current page as a favorite.
3. Press Ctrl, Shift and O to open your favorites, or Ctrl, Shift and B to show or hide the favorites bar.

## Chrome
1. Select the Bookmark star at the right end of the address bar to save this page.
2. Or press Ctrl and D to save it from the keyboard.
3. Press Ctrl, Shift and O to open the Bookmark Manager, or Ctrl, Shift and B to show or hide the bookmarks bar.

## Firefox
1. Press Ctrl and D to bookmark this page.
2. Press Ctrl and B to open the bookmarks sidebar, or Ctrl, Shift and O for the full bookmarks Library.
3. Press Ctrl, Shift and B to show or hide the bookmarks toolbar.
