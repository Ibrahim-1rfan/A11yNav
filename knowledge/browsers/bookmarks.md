## Edge
1. Edge calls bookmarks favorites. Press Ctrl and D to save the current page as a favorite.
2. Press Ctrl, Shift and O to open your favorites.
3. Press Ctrl, Shift and B to show or hide the favorites bar under the address bar.

## Chrome
1. Press Ctrl and D, or select the Bookmark star to the right of the address bar, to save the page.
2. Press Ctrl, Shift and B to show or hide the bookmarks bar.
3. Press Ctrl, Shift and O to open the Bookmark Manager. It is also under More, then Bookmarks and lists.

## Firefox
1. Press Ctrl and D to bookmark this page.
2. Press Ctrl and B to open the bookmarks sidebar, or Ctrl, Shift and O for the full bookmarks Library.
3. Press Ctrl, Shift and B to show or hide the bookmarks toolbar.
