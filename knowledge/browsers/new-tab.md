## Chrome
1. Select New tab, the plus sign next to the last tab at the top of the window.
2. Or press Ctrl and T to open a new tab from the keyboard.
3. To open a new window instead, press Ctrl and N.

## Edge
1. Select New tab, the plus sign next to the last tab at the top of the window.
2. Or press Ctrl and T to open a new tab and switch to it.
3. To open a new window instead, press Ctrl and N.

## Firefox
1. Select the plus sign on the right side of the last tab. The cursor appears in the address bar, ready for a search or web address.
2. Or press Ctrl and T to open a new tab from the keyboard.
3. To open a new window instead, press Ctrl and N.
