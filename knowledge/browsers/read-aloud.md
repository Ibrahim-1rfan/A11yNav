## Edge
1. Open the page you want to hear.
2. Press Ctrl, Shift and U together to start or stop Read aloud.
3. Or right-click anywhere on the page and choose Read aloud. It is also under Settings and more, then More tools, then Read aloud.
4. A toolbar appears at the top with play, pause, next and previous paragraph, and Voice options for speed and voice.
5. To stop, select Pause or the X on the Read aloud toolbar.

## Chrome
1. Open the page, then right-click it and select Open in reading mode.
2. In the reading mode panel, select Play to hear the text, and Pause to stop.
3. Use Previous and Next to jump between sentences, and Playback speed to go faster or slower.
4. To choose a different voice, select Settings, then Voice selection.

## Firefox
1. Open the article and press F9 to open Reader View.
2. Select the Read aloud (headphones) button on the left side.
3. Press play to listen. This only appears if Windows has a text-to-speech voice for the language of the article.
