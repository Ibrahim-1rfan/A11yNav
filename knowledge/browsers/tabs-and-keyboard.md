## Edge
1. New tab: Ctrl and T. Close the current tab: Ctrl and W.
2. Reopen the tab you just closed: Ctrl, Shift and T.
3. Next tab: Ctrl and Tab. Previous tab: Ctrl, Shift and Tab.
4. Go to the address bar to type a website: Ctrl and L, or Alt and D.
5. Full screen on or off: F11.

## Chrome
1. New tab: Ctrl and T. Close the current tab: Ctrl and W.
2. Reopen the tab you just closed: Ctrl, Shift and T.
3. Next tab: Ctrl and Tab, or Ctrl and Page Down. Previous tab: Ctrl, Shift and Tab. Jump to tab 1 to 8: Ctrl and the number.
4. Go to the address bar: Ctrl and L, Alt and D, or F6.
5. Full screen on or off: F11.

## Firefox
1. New tab: Ctrl and T. Close the current tab: Ctrl and W.
2. Reopen the last closed tab: Ctrl, Shift and T.
3. Jump to tab 1 to 8: Ctrl and the number.
4. Go to the address bar: Ctrl and L, Alt and D, or F6 pressed once.
5. Full screen on or off: F11.
