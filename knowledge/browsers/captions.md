## Chrome
1. Select More (three dots, top right), then Settings, then Accessibility.
2. Turn on Live Caption. Chrome then captions speech in videos and audio on any site. It runs on your computer and nothing is stored.
3. For another language, select Add languages next to Manage languages, then choose a Preferred caption language.
4. The captions appear in a bubble you can drag. Caption preferences sets their style.

## Edge
1. Open Settings and more, then Settings, then Accessibility.
2. Select Open caption preferences to set how captions look.
3. In Windows 11 you can also go to Start, Settings, Accessibility, Captions to choose a caption style.
