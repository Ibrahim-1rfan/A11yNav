# Klicky study results

30 students, 120 tasks.

- Tasks done without Klicky: 16 of 18 (89%)
- Tasks done with Klicky: 18 of 18 (100%)

Time spent with Klicky in the with-Klicky tasks (asking, waiting for it, listening), median per task: 15.2 s, Klicky used 1 time(s). It is left out of pauses and of the first-click time below; 'Time on task' includes it.

Each student's tasks are averaged per condition; the median across students is shown.
p is the exact two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test (with vs without, paired by student).
